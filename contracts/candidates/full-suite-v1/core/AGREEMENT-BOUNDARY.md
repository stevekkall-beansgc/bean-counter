## 4. Activation, replacement and revocation

Use existing AgreementActivate/AgreementActivationReplace/AgreementRevoke shapes. Customer is derived from exact accepted_binding_ref under scope; never inferred from a current head. Every explicit policy/product/fact_schema/plan copy must match that binding. The accepted binding need not be latest acceptance revision. Acceptance and activation counters are independent; publishing or accepting does not increment activation. Fresh transition requires expected activation revision and current administrative/accepted-source authority; no financial action or reassessment of existing targets.

New `ActivationTransition` (`workflow-activation`) closed record stores action activate|replace|revoke, head_transition_ref, binding_ref, effective_at, commit_sequence, accepted_at, and optional target_transition_ref required exactly for replace/revoke. Effective at_commit=trusted accepted time; scheduled time≥trusted accepted time. There is at most one not-yet-effective transition per agreement. New activate may follow only the latest committed effective boundary, with no competing pending schedule; use explicit replace to alter that pending selection. Equal accepted time is ordered by commit sequence; no retroactive insertion. Replace requires target equal the current unsuperseded pending transition and trusted time strictly before its effective_at. It appends a new immutable transition/activation-head revision; does not mutate/delete old rows. Revocation names current selected/pending transition via existing transition_ref, takes effect at commit and cancels future selection from that transition; it never reverses completed work or closes owed liabilities. To resume, a new authorized activation appends after the revoked boundary.

Binding validity is starts_at≤effective_at<ends_at when end exists. Fresh admission selection is greatest eligible unsuperseded `(effective_at,commit_sequence)` containing trusted acceptance time; revoke yields no new admission. Old invocation/chain/target terms remain pinned under original acceptance/activation even if later reports arrive; correction never resolves current activation. Cross-agreement/customer transition refs refuse. Revision CAS and immutable replacement edges make schedule order reproducible; a projection alone cannot certify selection.

HeadTransition action isn't inferred from timestamps: its exact new ActivationTransition supplies action. For activate/replace/revoke the head_kind is agreement_activation and accepted_binding_ref the binding referenced by that action. For revoke use that binding although selection becomes absent. Result_refs contain HeadTransition and ActivationTransition. Activation semantic key is its head transition key `(store,scope,customer,agreement,activation,new_revision)`; a new-key same-intent alias reuses original transition with no counter increment. Matching uses action/binding/explicit effective input/target/evidence, not newly computed at_commit time; store original intent projection. Stale expected revisions with changed intent conflict.
