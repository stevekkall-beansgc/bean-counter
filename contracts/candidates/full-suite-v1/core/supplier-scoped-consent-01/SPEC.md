## 1. Exact consent change and acyclic documents

Under this new registry ONLY, customer and platform payer/bearer accept the complete original AcceptanceTerms1; provider accepts its exact SupplierCommitment1 + SupplierOffer3 + SupplierTerms5, not unrelated retail prices. Prior Offer2.policy_ref, Terms4, Accept5 and full-digest consent remain strict on their original recipes. No stripping fields from old documents or reinterpretation of old assent.

SupplierCommitment1 is an immutable `workflow-offer` document with its own distinct schema `ledger-workflow-supplier-commitment/1`. Kind alone never makes it SupplierOffer3: exact schema and field role must agree. It contains scope/customer/agreement/supplier_slot and two closed projections: `program` independently extracted from the actual verified full plan and sources (§2); `acceptance` derived from actual normalized binding inputs (§3). SupplierOffer3 replaces whole policy_ref with exact commitment_ref; other original Offer2 constraints remain. SupplierTerms5 is Terms4's exact constraint dictionary plus commitment_ref, with schema/5 and offer_ref selecting only Offer3. All offer/terms/account/role/unit/exposure/source/time copies must agree. No offer creates assent or a supplier payable.

Graph: actual sources/full plan → derived commitment → Offer3 → Terms5 → AcceptanceTerms1 → fullterms assent. Provider assent → commitment/Offer3/Terms5. Commitment's acceptance.terms excludes schema, offer_ref and commitment_ref; every other Terms5 field is retained. This avoids a self-hash cycle without omitting supplier obligations. Offer-specific constraints (including interval and evidence) are independently bound by the provider's consent basis; they cannot exceed the commitment or source/terms bounds. Offer3 remains agreement-specific and consent does not transfer to another agreement/customer/store.

Accept6 retains existing Binding1/BindingInputs/AcceptanceTerms1 shapes. Its documents union adds new commitment/offer/terms/PartyAssent1; top-level assent_refs and binding_inputs.assent_refs must be identical canonical sets. Old retail Assent1 remains exactly customer registration verified. New provider/platform PartyAssent1 is a different schema in workflow-assent, never passed to the old customer-only validator. Authority_refs include actual original party authorizations and current head refs selected by the coordinator; normalized copies are compared, never trusted. The coordinator constructs SupplierConsentVerification1 and retains it with acceptance, referencing complete full plan/publication and exact independently matched commitment, assents, original/current party authorizations. It is verification DATA produced by the actual verifier, never a caller-success flag or reusable trusted cache certificate.
