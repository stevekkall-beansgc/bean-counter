The next candidate authority schema is `ledger-workflow-agreement-authority/2`. `AgreementAuthorityObservation` is a closed union whose branches are explicit `purpose:"admit"` or `purpose:"stop"`; the exact field dictionaries are in agreement-ready.schema.json. This branch has purpose admit and the prior fields unchanged otherwise. LocalOwnerAdmin alone remains insufficient; legacy source permissions do not substitute. **Stop:** only agreement.revoke, under S12 §7/15.1's authorized serialized admission-stop and separate activation-head CAS. The original accepted agreement/binding and its original assent/grant closure remain verified immutable history; no request can fabricate them or change original economic terms.

Missing/corrupt original evidence or source-state lineage is an integrity failure, not permission to manufacture a stop authority record. SourceState/current_grant_ref and revision must match retained current state; no old active state is relabeled current. All variants permit stop; none grants admission. The source observation is retained diagnostic/current-state evidence, not a renewed write permission requirement. Binding starts_at/ends_at do not gate stop, including pending future or expired bindings. Stop does not reverse work, cancel liabilities or shorten original correction/reporting rights.
