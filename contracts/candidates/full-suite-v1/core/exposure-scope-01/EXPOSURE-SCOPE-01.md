## 2. Exact aggregate and financial meaning

The identity of a retail exposure aggregate is `(logical_store_id, scope, original WorkClaim full Ref, original binding_slot)`. The WorkClaim is the authenticated permanent original business work identity, not transport key, current decision revision, policy label, newly named chain, or an arbitrary funding-leg name. A correction remains in the same original aggregate. All components charged to that original work/slot and all families associated with those original components are included, even when supplied by different permitted assessment operations. An independent original work has its own aggregate, including when the customer/agreement/slot is the same. Relabeling one original work cannot split its aggregate. Same original work charged to distinct slots is checked per slot plus existing book/role/total bounds; no cross-slot netting waives another ceiling.

For each original aggregate, prove a sound finite upper bound over all still-permitted simultaneous financial states under its original policy, quantities, windows, family alternatives, and correction rights. Root component nets retain their existing original base plus attached same-work modifier semantics. For a state, sum those applicable root nets plus its simultaneous positive contingent family obligations. Ordinary family discounts are checked separately against original component/binding discount capacity; they cannot buy premium headroom. A single exclusive family contributes at most its selected allowed alternative, not the sum of mutually exclusive codes. Distinct families which can coexist must be combined. Complete policy analysis may conservatively overapproximate reachable states, but cannot omit a possible obligation or a future reinstatement/correction.

A permitted conservative implementation may sum, over independent original roots, `max(original positive postings + simultaneous family premium maxima, permitted corrected positive postings after causal closure)`. Here same-work discounts are still independently validated and never change actual pricing; using gross positive postings as a bound is conservative, not a claim that the discounted bill equals that bound. Correlated quantity/source states must be respected or safely overapproximated. This paragraph authorizes no omitted family, speculative release, new amount or widened ceiling.

Use every applicable original Binding and SlotBindingTerms ceiling separately, as well as unchanged current/original source authority ceilings and existing book/role/quantity/discount guards. Do not flatten independent authority documents into a new authority. New scope does not redefine the purpose or permissions of SourceGrant.

The ceiling is an admission/authorization guard, not a pricing operator. Do not clip charges to it, rerate an original price, convert a discount to cash, or count all historical inverse/replacement postings as simultaneous liability. Actual actions remain computed with existing exact amounts/rounding. Current exposure and prospective upper bound must come from the same typed original graph and current state; no submitted amount, generic verified bit or fixture state array is an implementation operand.

Root correction can permanently close original families, so a reachable replacement-root state may exclude those families. Do not add maxima from mutually impossible pre/post-correction states as an exact maximum. A conservative bound may refuse new admission before any promise, but never authorize excess. The accepted proof must cover every promised future state at admission; later replay/correction cannot substitute a looser bound and deny an already accepted correction right. Current rights and original actual quantity/exposure constraints still apply. Unclaimed/none/zero does not release still-permitted future obligations. Only existing irrevocable closure/retirement rules remove a possibility, once. No expiry is extended, shortened or invented; no lifetime allowance is replenished. This rule does not waive all-ancestor correction decision/action/ref/work/byte/memory reservations or future supported-executor obligations.

Customer_local funding still verifies original mode, resource owner/payer and exact evidence. It is not an available cash account. Supplier/platform invocation conservation, supplier exposure ceilings, payment/settlement and other modes remain their unchanged separate full-scope obligations. No guarantee is inferred for an unimplemented mode.

## 5. Independent fixture recipe

Read `fixture-input.json` before author expected. All supplied finite states are conditional **specification test data**, not trusted runtime state or completeness proof. Amounts are integer scale18 atoms (small integers are literal atoms). For each work and each supplied state: B=sum(root_nets_atoms); P=sum over families of max(0, each alternative); D=sum over families of max(0, negated alternatives). State positive exposure is B+P; fixture discount validity is D<=B for the single common basis used by these cases. Work maximum is max of its state exposures. It passes iff maximum<=ceiling and all state discount checks pass. Independent works do not sum for the ceiling. Real distinct-root discounts additionally require each original root/role/binding constraint, which these small fixtures do not prove.

For terms bytes, use existing exact ASCII-JCS and H(domain,value)=SHA256(UTF8("bean-counter/"+domain+"/1") || NUL || JCS(value)). terms3 document is H(workflow-document,[schema,body]). No financial receipt/capacity/authentication is asserted.
