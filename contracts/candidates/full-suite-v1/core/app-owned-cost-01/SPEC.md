# App-owned attributable cost 01

Additive implementation of the approved APP-OWNED-CHAIN-REVIEW-20261017 (SHA256 e05627739695643add7522422ec646f93f6cad274ede3ffeae2e664a49014fea). Candidate until independent freeze. This changes the mandatory platform-funded path in CORE-AND-CONTRACTS §7: an app may charge its customer from actual authenticated, attributable final cost evidence without creating a supplier payable or requiring supplier participation. It does not reinterpret any old supplier offer, assent, invocation, line ownership, high-water, settlement, descriptor or journal. Those capabilities remain the separate optional AP module in the full suite. Full S01–S12 modes, BYOS and optional hosting remain backlog; this first capability is not full-suite qualification.

Existing formulas retain their exact meaning. This capability emits no supplier actions and rejects AP supplier rules, AP delayed declarations, AP invocations and implicit allocations before assent/admission. A later capability can combine fully supported modules; no old supplier promise is erased.

Policy3.app_delayed_costs contains complete AppCostDeclaration objects. Each is uniquely selected by (activity, component_key), names the exact platform resource_funding_leg separately from the retail customer-charge funding leg, the actual app work source, final-cost source, correction source, original usage field/unit, finalize/correction horizons and maximum final amount. It fixes `cost_basis=app_attributed_final_amount`, `comparable_quantity_rule=when_present_lte_original_observed`, `allocation=one_call_one_component`, `on_missing=wait`. All these operands must occur identically in original retail SlotBindingTerms6.app_cost_terms. The actual AppFormula rule/component/slot, declared source slots, original FactSchema field/unit, catalog/instantiation, funding and original/current ceilings must resolve exactly. New terms are retail, exposure_scope=original_work; no cash balance or AP budget is introduced.

A declaration requires exactly one final-cost rule for its named component, no duplicate call charge or hidden second consumer. The full compiler must detect all shared facts, context, modifiers, links, families and correction dependencies and support them truthfully. Unimplemented predicates/modes/allocations cannot silently evaluate with Null facts or be ignored. First support may refuse unimplemented compositions before accepting their promise. The independent service outcome in the proof depends only on the service component, not app cost, so cost correction does not reverse that outcome. Any real dependent outcome is included in actual closure.

AppBinding2, AppBindingInputs and AppAcceptanceTerms2 are additive copies of the existing binding/terms dictionaries with app_usage/app_final_cost evidence nominations allowed in source_slots. Fullterms digest is the existing H_workflow-acceptance-terms(body), now over the actual new schema body. It binds the complete publication, roles, source nominations, context, catalog/instantiation, terms Refs, funding, intervals, quantity/exposure and app cost declarations. Actual registered customer Assent1 and actual platform resource payer/bearer PartyAssent1 each cover these full terms. The retail action retains its original retail role and customer-charge funding leg. The declaration.resource_funding_leg separately names actual app cost funding, never silently replaces the retail payer. Platform resource funding names the actual platform resource owner/payer and retained accepted role basis, with no supplier_binding_slot in this app-only leg. BYOS/customer_api/customer_subscription/customer_local legs do not authorize platform app-cost pass-through. A separately declared genuine platform tool leg is independently checked; funding labels do not create cash/settlement authority.

## 2. Actual app attribution and finality

AppObservation1 is an immutable authenticated statement about one app call and one original accepted work/component. Its subject binds logical_store_id, scope, customer/agreement, exact original WorkRecord Ref and original AppBinding2 Ref, stable actual component slot/key and app_call {source_namespace,call_id}. The namespace is the original authenticated work source's canonical economic namespace, not a current assessor's namespace. The nominated work source must attest the observation through `evidence.retain` AppObserveRetain1; its original/current source grants authorize evidence.retain with submit+read, correct scope/customer/product/activity/source slot and app_usage evidence kind. Actual source enrollment/state/principal/root/time are obtained under the writer. Caller names and raw hashes are not authentication.

Original WorkRecord must be succeeded and retain the actual selected original quantity fact, schema and unit. Observation.quantity equals that fact's normalized nonnegative value and observation.usage_field/unit equals original declaration/schema. observed_at is the original actual work occurrence (explicit payload occurred_at or its existing trusted received-at fallback); it cannot be a later corrected work quantity. The backend source is accountable for truthful call attribution. The system verifies provenance/joins and does not claim physical measurement independent of that source.

Optional provider_key {provider,account,request_id} is an exact app-attested provider call identity where available, not supplier enrollment or assent. It has no authority beyond provenance and duplicate prevention. Missing provider quantity or provider key does not require supplier network participation. A backend cannot assert an aggregate invoice is exactly this call: first capability requires attributable per-call final amount, and unsupported aggregate/unallocated evidence remains outside financial finalization. There is no automatic allocation.

AppFinalCost1 is independently retained through AppCostRetain1 by the original nominated cost source (or the original correction source for cost_revision>1), using actual original/current evidence.retain submit+read rights and app_final_cost nomination. Its subject exactly equals the observation subject. observation_ref is exact, amount USD18 nonnegative, finality is `final_attributable_amount`, issued_at is no later than actual trusted retention time. A usage-only response, rate estimate, unsettled aggregate or arbitrary caller-computed price is not this final amount. Actual source material is retained in AppMaterial, nonempty strict canonical base64, decoded at most 4096 bytes, exact raw SHA256/media validation. This supports accountable evidence; it is not a provider signature or independent invoice verification claim.

quantity_comparison is a closed sum: comparable {quantity,unit} or unavailable {reason:not_reported|not_comparable}. Comparable quantity is nonnegative, same original unit, and <= original observation.quantity, and also within all original declared/invocation-free quantity ceilings. Accepted usage10/ceiling20 permits billed8 and10, refuses11 even though under20. Unavailable does not mean zero or successful comparison. It is a truthful disclosed status, not a workaround when actual comparable evidence exists. Source material/typed assertion consistency is an authenticated source responsibility plus mechanical verifier checks; same-source API/invoice agreement is not independent verification. No general conversion is inferred; an unsupported mapping is unavailable/not_comparable or refusal if exact attributable amount itself cannot be established.

The verifier constructs unchanged EvidenceVerification1 from actual principal/grant/revision/receipt time, attestation raw SHA/digest and exact subject binding refs: original work, original binding and (for final cost) observation. It uses `authenticated-nominated-source-retention/1`. The verification pair is consumed together and original origin is audited. App evidence cannot be substituted by the old supplier schema just because its numeric amount agrees.

## 3. Permanent claims and exact identities

Current same-source delivery identifiers do not replace original business call identity. Provider keys cannot be added/removed by a correction: they live in the immutable observation. A trusted dishonest source that fabricates a new physical call identity remains a source-truth threat, not something hashes prove absent.

Evidence retention enforces immutable subject/observation lineage and authentic original/current correction source. AppCostRevision1 points to the current decision, evidence and consumption; its logical head key is (logical_store_id,scope,full original claim Ref), CAS revision+exact prior Ref. It cannot migrate to another obligation. Zero cost retains the permanent claim and consumption.

Prospective stable app cost decision ID is `wdecision_`+H_workflow-decision([scope,original_work_record_id,component_slot,"app_cost"]). Its identity remains the existing H_workflow-dependency([ancestor_decision_id,dependent_decision_id,original_terms_ref]) tuple; all remaining body fields including exact target are protected by the record content digest; exact construction order and all-ancestor capacity follow the approved WorkRecord-target recipe. ## 4. Money, corrections and causal closure

`app_final_cost` uses the SAME checked rational kernel: eligible atoms from verified final evidence, multiply by original exact ratio, add original markup converted under existing Decimal/USD18 rules, one original round-nearest-ties-away at the original posting boundary; compare original ceiling and refuse excess, never clamp. Original policy/plan/rule/component/roles/funding/terms/fact context are authenticated and compiled, never selected from today's policy. AppObservation quantity constrains evidence eligibility, not a hidden alternative price formula. Any genuinely quantity/rate-derived cost mode needs its own explicit supported original policy definition; it is not enabled here.

cost.finalize/2 receives exact original work/component + observation and final evidence verification pairs. It requires current target/work status and all original/current finalization authority, original horizon and original capacity, then constructs AppCostClaim1, original retail action(s), EvaluationProof1, CostDecision3, consumption and revision. EvaluationProof uses the original WorkRecord facts/schema and accepted context, with the declared evidence basis/step under original compiled ordinals. It does not price Null facts while claiming predicates supported. Decision.kind=cost/status=active even for zero; no supplier action, supplier liability or payable is emitted.

cost.correct/2 resolves the permanent claim and current head; expected_revision must match. Replacement evidence must be the next final evidence of the SAME original observation, with all original/current correction rights and horizon, no later-work usage substitution. Recompute old/new under the SAME original program. Exact inverses of current active actions precede replacement actions in the existing canonical compiled posting recipe; zero has no nonzero active posting. Reconstruct/validate the complete old evaluation/actions/decision from their actual original origin, not merely a partial local amount comparison. Source restrictions/revocations are checked now; original receipts remain reproducible through historical verification plus current read permission.

Complete actual affected dependent closure and all ancestors are mandatory, including existing causal-A accepted reversal clauses. Root quantity correction/reversal may reverse a dependent app customer charge as authorized by the original causal terms; this does not alter the actual retained cost evidence or claim that a supplier debt vanished, because this mode creates no supplier debt. App revision becomes reversed, active actions empty, current evidence and consumed amount retained as provenance; no synthetic zero final evidence is needed for such customer-only inverse. It cannot later be resurrected under a new delivery or policy. Ordinary final-cost correction of an active claim always needs genuine replacement final evidence. Any independent service outcome stays unchanged; dependent outcomes follow exact original closure. No-success/deadline close, claimed-family prohibition, separate outcome token/target uniqueness and terminal family rules remain exactly the approved frozen recipes.

Original-work exposure includes all simultaneous positive original component/customer obligations and maximum permitted families/corrections per Terms6 and existing exposure amendment. Negative amounts do not create reserve credit. Corrected cost is bounded by accepted maximum_final_amount and formula/terms ceilings. All promised future app cost finalizations/corrections, evidence branches, rights, output sizes, actions and dependent closures must have implemented executors and complete source-derived resource/capacity coverage BEFORE the work is admitted. No default profile-max placeholders, deferred guarantees, new AP pool or new numeric cap.
