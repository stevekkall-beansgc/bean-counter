# Phase 4 supplier comparison foundation — incomplete milestone

**PHASE-4 FOUNDATION ONLY. This is not a Phase 4 exit.**

The private local comparison facade connects the existing verified supplier
chronology to the nonposting core driver and concrete SQLite/PostgreSQL readers.
It compares 2–8 amount tables over one fixed booked final base and the complete
retained registration → ordinary → correction → closure prefix. Source bytes,
original receipts, parties, currency/scale, family membership, authority,
evidence, windows, limits and supplier terms remain unchanged.

The existing acceptance path supports supplier outcomes only. Consequently the
observed supplier economics are identical in every allowed candidate. Retail
amount alternatives are shown as **unobserved**: no retail outcome activity,
earned retail adjustment, new eligibility or forecast is inferred. In the actual
store fixture the booked retail basis is 10000 USD atoms; the supplier base is
3000, ordinary fee +2500, correction −2500, and closure has no monetary delta.
Consumed capacity does not replenish on correction; closure releases held capacity.

## Private entry and boundaries

`service::comparison::foundation::compare_retained` acquires process-wide admission,
requires trusted current scope/disclosure authorization, loads one stable complete
read-only snapshot, closes it, and drives bounded comparison with cooperative
cancellation. Its `FoundationReport` has private bytes and no acceptance conversion.
A streaming 4 MiB limit prevents partial output. The original numeric control must
match retained monetary history and every supplier reservation receipt before a
report is returned. Each candidate has its own state; infeasible candidates have
no comparable prefix total. Reports contain allowlisted estimates, original
historical references and provenance, never new canonical records or intentions.

Existing read-only database enforcement and attempted-statement tracing are tested
separately from deliberately rejected mutation probes. Current host authorization
is a mandatory trusted local seam, not a public authentication service. Reports
are private synthetic/local observations and do not establish assent or authority.
