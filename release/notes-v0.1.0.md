# Bean Counter 0.1.0 — local SQLite billing

Bean Counter records fixed-price customer work, authorized outcome adjustments and corrections in a local SQLite installation. It retains durable receipts, prevents duplicate billing across retries, explains retained decisions, manages revocable local permissions and exports complete reconciled JSON statements. The executable remains `ledger`; the implementation was developed as Ledger Lab.

One customer/agreement per installation; explicit immutable operator-supplied terms and authority evidence; fixed USD pricing and one fixed-amount outcome family. Users supply and operate their machine, memory, storage and backups. Follow the billing quickstart and quiescent whole-installation recovery procedure. No service account, model API, mandatory runtime subscription or payment processor is required.

Resource guarantees are deferred: no aggregate RAM/disk reservation, capacity proof, saturation-completion guarantee or guaranteed completion of accepted offline work under reserved resources. Existing bounds and pricing/authority/persistence guards remain enforced. Unknown results require original-identity retry or reconciliation; they are never evidence of rollback. Small walkthrough measurements are not hardware minimums or throughput guarantees.

PostgreSQL product support, multi-host writer replacement, advanced comparison, remote authentication and hosted operation remain deferred. Statements are not tax/legal invoices and no money is collected. Historical full-platform specifications and failed or incomplete gates retain their status. See `CURRENT-REQUIREMENTS.md` for the authoritative supported/deferred matrix.

Independent acceptance remains platform-blocked. Validation is author-run; this release does not establish full original G2/all-16-R3 acceptance or production readiness. Native evidence covers macOS 26.6.2/Apple silicon only. No MSRV, clean-account download launch, signing/notarization, reproducible build or signed CI artifact provenance is claimed.

Local billing contract version: `v0.1`, independent of the preserved frozen economic profile versions. Exact released source, remote CI evidence and artifact checksums belong in the final GitHub Release body and attached manifest. No customer installations, private evidence databases, credentials or session dumps are included.
