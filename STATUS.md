# Bean Counter status

Bean Counter's current public product is the **local SQLite billing CLI**. The [v0.3.0 release](https://github.com/stevekkall-beansgc/bean-counter/releases/tag/v0.3.0) adds guided terminal setup and provides unsigned native packages tested on macOS 26.6.2 with Apple silicon and Ubuntu 24.04.5 x86-64/glibc 2.39. The packages include checksums, build provenance and dependency inventories. Other OS versions and Linux distributions, clean-account launch, signing and production host certification have not been verified; Windows is deferred.

The v0.4.2 release published the compatibility policy. The v0.4.3 documentation release added the owner-approved [billing roadmap](docs/billing-roadmap.md) and [M1 qualification record](docs/m1-current-format-qualification.md). The v0.5.0 release implements M2 customers and agreements, v0.6.0 completes M3 continuous history and v0.7.0 completes M4 exact per-work usage for their named source-built profiles. The v0.8.0 source candidate implements M5: explicit schema 11, customer billing periods, cumulative usage and corrections, post-close adjustments, recurrence, fiscal reporting, immutable statement/4 close and finance export/4. M0 through M4 are complete within the boundaries recorded in the [canonical billing roadmap](docs/billing-roadmap.md); M5 publication remains pending the exact-commit remote release gates and tag recorded in the [M5 qualification](docs/m5-qualification.md). The v0.3.0 release remains the latest native-package release; native artifacts are not qualified for M2–M5.

The reduced local SQLite Phase 4 and the finance CSV Phase 5 are complete. The amended local Phase 6 is complete **with owner acceptance**. The owner waived its separate independent-review requirement; no independent technical review was completed or passed for this local release. Author validation and the owner's earlier synthetic v0.2.0 walkthrough are distinct from independent review. This status does not certify the original full platform plan.

The supported profile is one operator-controlled business per private installation, with multiple explicit customers and immutable customer agreements. The guided setup asks for actual terms and evidence without hand-authored JSON; unattended products retain the strict JSON interface. It retains fixed-price and scale-18 work, authorized outcomes and corrections, customizable billing periods, cumulative activity, explicit recurrence, post-close adjustments, fiscal reports, durable receipts, immutable statements and reconciled finance CSV exports. It does not obtain customer consent, run recurrence in the background, collect payment or guarantee downstream ingestion. Users provide and protect the machine, storage, backups and recovery process. Admission ceilings remain refusal limits, not capacity reservations or completion guarantees; measured M3/M5 synthetic evidence does not establish performance at the ceilings.

PostgreSQL product support, multi-host operation, remote authentication, hosting, automatic payments, resource guarantees and advanced comparison remain deferred. Historical PostgreSQL tests and design documents do not establish support for this billing profile. See [current requirements](CURRENT-REQUIREMENTS.md), the [billing quickstart](docs/billing-quickstart.md), [finance walkthrough](docs/finance-e2e.md) and [release profile](release/local-sqlite.md) for exact behavior and limitations.
