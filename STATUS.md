# Bean Counter status

Bean Counter's current public product is the **local SQLite billing CLI**. The [v0.2.1 release](https://github.com/stevekkall-beansgc/bean-counter/releases/tag/v0.2.1) provides source and an unsigned native archive tested on macOS 26.6.2 with Apple silicon. The archive includes checksums, build provenance, a dependency inventory and an installed walkthrough. Other operating systems, clean-account launch, signing and production host certification have not been verified.

The reduced local SQLite Phase 4 and the finance CSV Phase 5 are complete. The amended local Phase 6 is complete **with owner acceptance**. The owner waived its separate independent-review requirement; no independent technical review was completed or passed for this local release. Author validation and the owner's earlier synthetic v0.2.0 walkthrough are distinct from independent review. This status does not certify the original full platform plan.

The supported profile is one operator-controlled customer and agreement per private installation. It retains explicit terms and evidence, fixed-price work, authorized outcomes and corrections, durable receipts, complete statements and reconciled finance CSV exports. It does not obtain customer consent, collect payment or guarantee that a downstream finance system ingests an export. Users provide and protect the machine, storage, backups and recovery process. The hard admission ceilings include 1,000 accepted decisions, 1,000 aliases, 1,000 permission changes, 32 MiB of economic payloads and a separate 32 MiB of alias ingress; these are limits, not capacity reservations or completion guarantees.

PostgreSQL product support, multi-host operation, remote authentication, hosting, automatic payments, resource guarantees and advanced comparison remain deferred. Historical PostgreSQL tests and design documents do not establish support for this billing profile. See [current requirements](CURRENT-REQUIREMENTS.md), the [billing quickstart](docs/billing-quickstart.md), [finance walkthrough](docs/finance-e2e.md) and [release profile](release/local-sqlite.md) for exact behavior and limitations.
