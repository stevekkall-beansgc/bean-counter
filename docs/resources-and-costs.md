# Resources and costs — local SQLite billing

Measured 2026-09-23. This manifest covers the `ledger billing` profile; the old Phase 4/R3 worksheets are not its hardware requirements. No verified production minimum, maximum-volume performance result or vendor monthly quote is claimed.

The measured workload ran sequentially and reopened the database for every command. It independently reconciled 20 × 250 - 50 + 50 = 5,000 atoms; exact and renamed retries added no charge. A complete quiescent copy reopened with identical statement and saved retry receipt. A detailed local command/evidence transcript accompanies the delivery checkpoint. The first measurement wrapper failed because macOS restricted its timing-system query after a successful initialization; that directory was preserved, and measurement used a fresh installation with native child resource accounting instead. No failed initialization was silently reused.

Admission ceilings: 1,000 accepted decisions, 1,000 aliases, 1,000 permission changes, 32 MiB economic payloads and separately 32 MiB alias ingress. Setup/permission inputs are at most 64 KiB; event input 256 KiB; individual economic bundles 8 MiB. Permission input also bounds its reason to 8 KiB. These bounds limit work; they do not reserve disk or RAM. Complete-history replay/reporting can become slower as history grows, especially corrections. Measure the intended workload and provision free disk/RAM before reliance; untested hardware and operating systems remain outside this native evidence.

If memory, disk, locks or storage interfaces are unavailable, operations may refuse or report uncertainty. They must not convert an uncertain commit to success/rollback. Resolve original identities after reopen; preserve integrity failures for investigation. Customer obligations include accurate terms/authority assertions, account and private-path protection, local storage honoring locks/fsync, backup verification, resource monitoring and post-restore reconciliation. There are no runtime service credentials to renew in this profile.
